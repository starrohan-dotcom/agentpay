# YouTube Video Strategy: AgentPay Launch

To hit 10,000 users, we need to create content that ranges from "Mind-Blowing Short-form" to "Deep-Dive Educational."

## 1. The "Magic" Viral Short (60 Seconds)
**Hook:** "I just gave my AI a bank account. Watch this."
**Core Value:** Zero-code onboarding for Claude Desktop.
**Goal:** High-volume traffic and mass awareness.
**CTA:** "npx agentpay"

## 2. The Builder deep-dive (10-15 Minutes)
**Title:** Building the first Self-Sustaining Agent with AgentPay.
**Core Value:** Practical tutorial showing LangChain + Base Mainnet.
**Goal:** Converting serious developers and building a community.
**CTA:** GitHub Stars and Showcase submissions.

## 3. The Enterprise Pitch (5-8 Minutes)
**Title:** Why the future of AI needs Safe (ERC-7579) Smart Accounts.
**Core Value:** Discussing security, spending limits, and USDC.
**Goal:** Establishing authority and attracting high-value/enterprise users.
**CTA:** Read the Litepaper.

## 4. The "Social Proof" Roundup
**Title:** 5 Cool Agents built with AgentPay.
**Core Value:** Showcasing the community showcase.
**Goal:** Fostering a network effect and encouraging new PRs.
**CTA:** Submit your agent.
